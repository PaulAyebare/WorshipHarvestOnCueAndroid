# Worship Harvest OnCue Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 10, 2026**

Worship Harvest OnCue is an Android app for planning worship services and playing audio. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores service names, dates and times, section order, playback settings, service notes, audio labels, and the audio files you import in the app's private storage. This information is used to provide the app's features. The app does not require an account and does not send service plans, notes, or imported audio to the developer. The app does not provide cloud sync or automatic transfer between devices.

When you choose an audio file, Android's system document picker gives the app access to that selected file. Worship Harvest OnCue copies the file into the selected service so it can play offline. If you select a file through Google Drive, Android and the Drive document provider handle access to that file under Google's terms and privacy practices. The app does not ask for your Google password or receive your Google account profile.

You can remove an imported file from its audio editor or delete a service and its files from the app. The app does not enable Android app-data backup. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data.

## Data collection, analytics, and advertising

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not collect personal information for the developer or share service data with the developer. Audio is accessed only when you select a file to import.

## Children

The app does not request personal information from children and does not provide public profiles, messaging, or social features. Service plans and imported audio are stored locally on the device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same public policy address with a revised effective date.
